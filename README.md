# EUI Totem Timer Style

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

**Read your totem timers at a glance.**

When several totems are active, small countdown numbers can be easy to miss. EUI Totem Timer Style gives EUI's totem timers a clear gold color, a thick black outline, and text sized to stay readable as you adjust your icons.

- **Clearer countdowns.** Gold text, a bold black outline, a subtle shadow, and an on-screen size of approximately 14 points.
- **One style across your totems.** Covers the active totem bar and the Shaman Call Totem bar's existing countdowns.
- **Keep your chosen font.** Follows the font selected for EUI Resource Bars and adapts to icon size changes.

For WoW Forever with EUI Resource Bars. Install and enable the addon alongside EUI. If your totem bar and its timer display are already enabled, the style applies automatically—no extra setup is needed. Continue using EUI's own options to choose your font and show or hide timers.

## 简体中文

**图腾一摆满，剩余时间也要一眼看清。**

多个图腾同时生效时，细小的倒计时数字容易被忽略。EUI Totem Timer Style 为 EUI 图腾计时器换上醒目的金黄色、粗黑描边和更清晰的字号，让你更容易掌握图腾的剩余时间。

- **倒计时更醒目。** 金黄色文字、粗黑描边与轻微阴影，实际显示字号约为 14 号。
- **两种图腾条统一风格。** 覆盖已激活的图腾条，以及萨满召唤图腾条原有的倒计时。
- **沿用你选好的字体。** 跟随 EUI 资源条的字体选择，并根据图标大小调整文字显示。

适用于 WoW Forever 的 EUI 资源条。与 EUI 一同安装并启用即可；如果已经开启图腾条及计时显示，样式会自动生效，无需额外设置。字体选择和计时显示开关继续使用 EUI 原有选项。
